Speaker Setup

Welcome to the Speaker Setup

This will install Speaker 1.0.0 on your Android phone.

Speaker isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time:

  1. Download the file. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Speaker and follow the Setup Guide.

Click Download to continue.

Updating

Download the newer version from this page and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall first: your settings, keys and permissions are kept. After updating, open Speaker and tap Start floating bubble again.

Release notes
Speaker 1.0.0 — 22 November 2026
  • AI rewrite uses a draft card: your words collect beside the bubble, you can edit them, and the whole text is rewritten at once when you tap Send.
  • Long-press the bubble for a quick menu to switch AI rewrite on or off, or open the app.
  • When no text box is selected, the result is copied to the clipboard.
  • Recent dictations: optionally keep your last 50 dictations and their AI versions on the phone.
  • Model lists load from each provider and show only the models your key can use.
