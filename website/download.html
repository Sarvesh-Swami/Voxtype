Installing the app

  1. Tap Download above. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install. Android asks you to allow Install unknown apps for your browser the first time. Tap Settings, switch it on, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect says the app is from an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway. This appears because Speaker isn't on Google Play yet.
  5. Open Speaker and follow the setup guide (about two minutes).
Android 13 and later: because the app was installed from a website, the Speaker auto-type switch in Accessibility may be greyed out at first. The setup guide shows the one-time fix.

Updating

To update, download the newer version from this page and install it over the old one. Don't uninstall first: your settings, keys and permissions stay as they are. After an update, open Speaker and tap Start floating bubble again.
