Installing Speaker

Because Speaker is installed from this website rather than Google Play, Android asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time.

  1. Download the file. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browser. When prompted, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an unrecognised app, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Speaker and follow the setup guide. It takes about two minutes.
Android 13 and later: for apps installed from a website, the Speaker auto-type switch in Accessibility starts out restricted. The setup guide shows how to allow it.

Updating

Download the newer version from this page and install it over the current one. Don't uninstall first: your settings, keys and permissions are kept. After updating, open Speaker and tap Start floating bubble again.

Speaker 1.0.0
