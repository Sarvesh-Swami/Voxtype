Installing Speaker

Speaker isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to confirm a couple of things the first time you install it from a website.

  1. Download the file using the button above. If your browser asks whether to keep it, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installs from your browser. When asked, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks about an unrecognised app, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open Speaker and follow the Setup Guide. It takes about two minutes.
Android 13 and later: the Speaker auto-type switch in Accessibility starts out restricted for apps installed from a website. The Setup Guide shows the one-time fix.

Updating

Download the newer version from this page and install it over the one you have. Don't uninstall first: your settings, keys and permissions are kept. After updating, open Speaker and tap Start floating bubble again.
