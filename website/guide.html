Permissions

When you first open Speaker, the Finish setup card lists four steps. Tap each one in turn.

StepWhat to doWhy it's needed
MicrophoneTap Allow and choose While using the app.To hear you while you dictate.
Display over appsTap Allow, find Speaker in the list and switch it on.Shows the floating bubble.
Auto-typingTap Enable, read the explanation, tap Agree & continue, then open Speaker auto-type and switch it on.Types your words into the text box you're using.
Run in backgroundTap Allow and choose Unrestricted or Don't optimise.Stops the phone from closing Speaker.

Auto-typing is optional. Without it, Speaker copies what you said to the clipboard so you can paste it yourself. In Accessibility settings, Speaker auto-type may be listed under Installed apps or Downloaded apps.

Restricted settings (Android 13 and later)

Android protects Accessibility for apps installed from outside Google Play. If the Speaker auto-type switch is greyed out, or you see a Restricted setting message:

  1. Try to switch it on once so Android shows the message, then close it.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Speaker.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner and choose Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  4. Return to Settings → Accessibility → Speaker auto-type and switch it on.

Background and battery

Some phones close apps in the background to save battery. When that happens to Speaker, the bubble disappears and Android also switches auto-typing off.

Realme, Oppo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo and Samsung phones: as well as setting battery use to Unrestricted, turn on Auto launch or Autostart for Speaker if your phone offers it, and lock Speaker in the recent-apps screen (open Recents, then long-press Speaker or pull it down and choose Lock).

Speech engines

In the Speech engine section, choose who turns your voice into text.

EngineKeyNotes
Android built-inNot neededFree. Uses your phone's own speech recognition.
GroqRequiredFast Whisper models. Free tier available.
OpenAIRequiredAccurate transcription models.
Google GeminiRequiredGemini models.
DeepgramRequiredNova models.
CustomRequiredAny OpenAI-compatible server. Enter its address.

For an online engine, create an API key on the provider's website and paste it in API keys. Tap Refresh next to Model to load the models your key can use. Leave Language blank to detect it automatically, or enter a code such as en or hi. Providers charge for usage under their own terms; Speaker itself is free.

The floating bubble

Tap Start floating bubble in the app. The bubble appears at the edge of the screen and a Speaker notification is shown while it runs.

  1. Open any app and tap into a text box.
  2. Tap the bubble and speak. Each phrase is typed when you pause.
  3. Tap it again to stop.
ActionResult
TapStart or stop dictation
DragMove the bubble
Long-pressQuick menu: turn AI rewrite on or off, or open Speaker

You can also use the Dictate button in the notification, or add the Dictate tile to Quick Settings: pull down twice, tap the edit (pencil) icon and drag the tile in.

AI rewrite

AI rewrite cleans up what you said, such as punctuation, filler words and grammar, or follows your own instructions. It uses the provider you choose: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or a custom server.

  1. In AI rewrite, switch it on, pick a provider and model, and add that provider's key under API keys. Edit the session prompt to change how text is rewritten.
  2. Tap the bubble and speak. Nothing is typed yet: your words collect in a draft card beside the bubble.
  3. Tap the text in the card to correct anything with the keyboard.
  4. Tap Send, or the bubble. The bubble shows Processing, then the rewritten text is typed into your text box. If no text box is selected, it's copied to the clipboard.
Draft cardResult
✕Discard the draft
MoreKeep talking and add to the draft
SendRewrite the whole draft. If it fails, the draft is kept and the button changes to Retry.

Recent dictations

With AI rewrite on, switch on Keep recent dictations on this phone to keep your last 50 dictations alongside their AI versions. Open Recent dictations to review or copy either version. They are stored only on your phone, and switching the setting off clears them.
