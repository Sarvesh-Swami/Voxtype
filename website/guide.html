Speaker Setup Guide

Everything you need to get Speaker running, from the first launch to AI rewrite.

Permissions

When you first open Speaker, the Finish setup card lists four steps. Tap each one in turn.

StepWhat to doWhy
MicrophoneTap Allow and choose While using the app.To hear you while you dictate.
Display over appsTap Allow, find Speaker in the list and switch it on.Shows the floating bubble.
Auto-typingTap Enable, read the explanation, tap Agree & continue, then open Speaker auto-type and switch it on.Types your words into the text box.
Run in backgroundTap Allow and choose Unrestricted or Don't optimise.Stops the phone closing Speaker.

Auto-typing is optional. Without it, Speaker copies what you said to the clipboard. In Accessibility settings, Speaker auto-type may be listed under Installed apps or Downloaded apps.

Restricted settings (Android 13 and later)

Android protects Accessibility for apps installed from outside Google Play. If the Speaker auto-type switch is greyed out:

  1. Try to switch it on once so Android shows the message, then close it.
  2. Open Settings > Apps > Speaker.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  4. Go back to Settings > Accessibility > Speaker auto-type and switch it on.
Background and battery

Some phones close apps in the background to save battery. When that happens, the bubble disappears and Android also switches auto-typing off.

Realme, Oppo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo and SamsungSet battery use to Unrestricted, turn on Auto launch or Autostart for Speaker if your phone has it, and lock Speaker in the recent-apps screen.
Speech engines
EngineKeyNotes
Android built-inNot neededFree. Uses your phone's own speech recognition.
GroqRequiredFast Whisper models. Free tier available.
OpenAIRequiredAccurate transcription models.
Google GeminiRequiredGemini models.
DeepgramRequiredNova models.
CustomRequiredAny OpenAI-compatible server. Enter its address.

For an online engine, create an API key on the provider's website and paste it under API keys. Tap Refresh beside Model to load the models your key can use. Leave Language blank to detect it automatically. Providers charge for usage under their own terms; Speaker itself is free.

The floating bubble

Tap Start floating bubble. The bubble appears at the edge of the screen and a Speaker notification shows while it runs.

ActionResult
TapStart or stop dictation
DragMove the bubble
Long-pressQuick menu: switch AI rewrite on or off, or open Speaker

You can also use the Dictate button in the notification, or add the Dictate tile to Quick Settings.

AI rewrite
  1. In AI rewrite, switch it on, pick a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or custom) and model, and add the key under API keys.
  2. Tap the bubble and speak. Your words collect in a draft card beside the bubble.
  3. Tap the text in the card to fix anything with the keyboard.
  4. Tap Send, or the bubble. The bubble shows Processing, then the rewritten text is typed in, or copied to the clipboard if no text box is selected.
Draft cardResult
✕Discard the draft
MoreKeep talking and add to the draft
SendRewrite the whole draft. If it fails, the draft is kept and the button changes to Retry.
Recent dictations

With AI rewrite on, switch on Keep recent dictations on this phone to keep your last 50 dictations with their AI versions, ready to copy. They're stored only on your phone, and switching the setting off clears them.
