Setup Guide

Setup takes about two minutes. When you open Speaker, the Finish setup card lists four steps. Tap each one and follow the notes below.

On this page
  1. Give Speaker its permissions
  2. Greyed-out auto-type switch (Android 13+)
  3. Choose a speech engine
  4. Start the bubble and dictate
  5. Using AI rewrite

1. Give Speaker its permissions

Setup stepWhat to doWhy
MicrophoneTap Allow, then choose While using the app.To hear you while you dictate.
Display over appsTap Allow, find Speaker in the list and switch it on.Shows the floating bubble.
Auto-typingTap Enable, read the explanation and tap Agree & continue. In Accessibility, open Speaker auto-type (sometimes under Installed apps or Downloaded apps) and switch it on.Types your words into the text box you're using.
Run in backgroundTap Allow and choose Unrestricted or Don't optimize for Speaker.Stops the phone from closing the bubble.

Auto-typing is optional. Without it, Speaker copies what you said to the clipboard so you can paste it.

Realme, Oppo, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo and Samsung phones may still close Speaker in the background, and that also switches off auto-typing. As well as Unrestricted battery, turn on Auto launch (or Autostart) for Speaker if your phone has it, and lock Speaker in the recent-apps screen (open Recents, then long-press or pull down on Speaker and choose Lock).

2. Greyed-out auto-type switch (Android 13+)

Android 13 and later protect Accessibility for apps installed from outside Google Play. If the Speaker auto-type switch is greyed out or you see Restricted setting:

  1. Try to switch it on once, so Android shows the restricted-setting message, and close it.
  2. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Speaker.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right and choose Allow restricted settings. Confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  4. Go back to Settings → Accessibility → Speaker auto-type and switch it on.

3. Choose a speech engine

In the Speech engine card, pick who turns your voice into text:

EngineKey needed?Notes
Android built-inNoFree. Uses your phone's own speech recognition. A good place to start.
GroqYesFast Whisper models. Groq offers a free tier.
OpenAIYesAccurate transcription models.
Google GeminiYesGemini models.
DeepgramYesNova models.
CustomYesAny OpenAI-compatible server. Enter its URL.

For the online engines, create a key on the provider's website and paste it in the API keys card. Tap Refresh next to Model to load the models your key can use. You pay the provider for what you use, under their own terms. Speaker itself is free.

4. Start the bubble and dictate

  1. Tap Start floating bubble. The mic bubble appears at the edge of the screen and a Speaker notification is shown.
  2. Open any app and tap into a text box.
  3. Tap the bubble and speak. Each phrase is typed when you pause.
  4. Tap the bubble again to stop.
Bubble actionWhat it does
TapStart or stop dictation
DragMove the bubble anywhere
Long-pressQuick menu: turn AI rewrite on or off, or open Speaker

You can also start dictation from the Dictate button in the notification, or add the Dictate tile to Quick Settings (pull down twice, tap the pencil icon and drag the tile in).

5. Using AI rewrite

AI rewrite tidies what you said: punctuation, filler words, grammar, or anything else you put in your own prompt. It uses an AI provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or a custom server) with your own key.

  1. In the AI rewrite card, switch it on, pick a provider and model, and add that provider's key in API keys. You can edit the session prompt to change how the text is rewritten.
  2. Tap the bubble and speak. Nothing is typed yet: your words collect in a draft card next to the bubble.
  3. Tap the text in the card to fix a word or two with the keyboard.
  4. Tap ✨ Send (or the bubble). The bubble shows Processing while the AI works, then the rewritten text is typed into your text box. If no text box is selected, it's copied to the clipboard.
Draft card buttonWhat it does
✕Throw the draft away
🎤 MoreKeep talking and add to the draft
✨ SendSend the whole draft to the AI. Shows Retry if it fails, and your draft is kept.

Turn on Keep recent dictations on this phone to see your last 50 dictations and their AI versions under Recent dictations, and copy either one. They never leave your phone.
