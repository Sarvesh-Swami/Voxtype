Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

App: Speaker (Android, package app.speaker.dictate). Developer: [DEVELOPER NAME]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Summary

Speaker is a voice-typing app. It has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data. What you say is processed either by your phone's own speech service or by an AI provider that you choose and connect with your own API key.

What the app handles and where it goes

DataWhyWhere it goes
Microphone audioTo turn your speech into text, only while you are dictating.Built-in engine: your phone's speech recognition service (provided by Android / Google on most phones), on the device where supported. API engine (optional): each spoken phrase is sent over HTTPS directly from your phone to the provider you selected (OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini, Deepgram, or a custom OpenAI-compatible URL you enter). Audio is not stored by the app; temporary phrase files are deleted after sending.
Transcribed textTo type it into the text field you are using.Stays on your phone. If you turn on AI rewrite, your words are first collected on your phone, where you can edit them; when you tap Send, that text and your prompt are sent to the AI provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, or your custom URL).
Recent dictations (optional)So you can review what you said and what the AI wrote, and copy either.When AI rewrite and "Keep recent dictations" are on, your last 50 dictations and their AI versions are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Never sent anywhere, excluded from backup and device transfer. Clear them any time in the app; turning the setting off also clears them.
API keys and settingsTo call the providers you chose.Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. A key is only sent to the provider it belongs to.
Diagnostic logTroubleshooting.Kept in memory on your phone. It is only shared if you tap Copy or Share logs. It does not contain your dictated words or API keys.

Providers process the data you send them under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the policy of any provider you connect.

Permissions

  • Microphone: hears you while you dictate. A notification is shown whenever the dictation service is running.
  • Accessibility service ("Speaker auto-type"): types your dictated words into the focused text field in any app, the way a keyboard does. It is not used to read, collect, store or share screen content, passwords or anything you type, and no data from it leaves your phone. You turn it on yourself after an in-app explanation, and can turn it off any time in Settings → Accessibility.
  • Display over other apps: shows the floating microphone bubble.
  • Notifications / foreground service: keeps dictation available and shows its controls.
  • Internet: only used to reach the API providers you configure.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages or files requested) for security and to keep the site running.

Sharing and selling

The developer does not sell or share your data and has no access to it. Data only leaves your phone to go to the speech or AI providers you choose, as described above.

Children

Speaker is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

Use the built-in engine and leave AI rewrite off to avoid sending anything to third-party AI providers. Delete your API keys in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
