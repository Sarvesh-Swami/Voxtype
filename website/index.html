What Speaker does

●

Floating mic bubble

Sits on top of every app. Tap to start, tap to stop, drag it anywhere on the screen.

✎

Types where your cursor is

Your words land in the focused text box. If an app doesn't accept typing, they go to the clipboard.

∞

Talk as long as you like

Dictation keeps going, phrase by phrase, until you tap again. It doesn't stop when you pause.

✨

Optional AI rewrite

Say it your way, check and edit the draft, then tap Send. An AI tidies the whole thing at once.

⚙

Pick your speech engine

Use your phone's free built-in recognizer, or connect OpenAI, Groq, Gemini or Deepgram with your own key.

★

Many ways to start

The bubble, a Quick Settings tile, the notification button, or the big button in the app.

How it works

  1. Tap the bubble while you're in any app. It turns red and starts listening.
  2. Speak normally. Speaker types each phrase into your text box as you pause.
  3. Tap again to stop. That's it. No switching apps, no copy and paste.

With AI rewrite turned on, nothing is typed while you talk. Your words collect in a small card next to the bubble, where you can fix them. Tap ✨ Send (or the bubble) and the cleaned-up text is typed in one go. When no text box is selected, it's saved to the clipboard.

Your words stay yours

  • No account, no sign-up, no ads and no analytics.
  • We have no servers. Nothing you say is ever sent to us.
  • The built-in engine needs no key at all. Online engines are only used if you add your own key.
  • Keys and settings stay in the app's private storage on your phone.
