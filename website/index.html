Features

Everything you need to type with your voice

Simple enough to use in seconds, flexible enough to fit the way you work.

Floating microphone

A bubble that sits over every app. Tap to start, tap to stop, and drag it wherever you like.

Types where you are

Words go straight into the text box you're using. If an app won't accept typing, they go to the clipboard.

Talk as long as you like

Speaker keeps listening through pauses, so you can stop and think without losing your place.

AI rewrite

Say it your way, check the draft, and send it once for clean, punctuated text in your own style.

Your choice of engine

Use your phone's free speech recognition, or connect the provider you prefer with your own key.

Quick to start

From the bubble, the notification, a Quick Settings tile or the big button in the app.

How it works

Up and talking in two minutes

1

Install Speaker

Download the app and follow the quick setup card: microphone, bubble and auto-typing.

2

Open any app

Messages, email, notes, a browser. Tap into the box where you want to write.

3

Tap and talk

Tap the bubble and speak. Your words appear as you pause. Tap again when you're done.

Say it messy.
Send it polished.

Turn on AI rewrite and your words collect in a little draft card next to the bubble. Fix anything you like, tap Send, and the whole thing is rewritten at once.

  • Keeps the full context of what you said
  • Edit the draft before it goes anywhere
  • Your own instructions: tone, format, language
  • OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq or your own server

Privacy

Your words stay yours

Speaker has no servers, so we never receive your voice, your text or your keys. Your speech goes only to your phone's own service or to the provider you choose.

  • No account or sign-up
  • No ads, no analytics
  • Keys stay on your phone
  • You choose every provider
