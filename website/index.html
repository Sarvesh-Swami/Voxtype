Type Without the Keyboard

Speaker works over the top of whatever you're using, so there's no keyboard to switch and nothing to paste. Tap to start, tap to stop, and drag the bubble wherever it suits you. Pause as long as you like: Speaker keeps listening until you tell it to stop. In the rare app that won't accept typing, your words go to the clipboard instead.

Say It Your Way, Send It Polished

Turn on AI rewrite and your words collect in a small draft card beside the bubble instead of being typed straight away. Read it, fix a word, then tap Send. The whole draft is rewritten at once, so the AI sees everything you said, and the clean version is typed in for you. Write your own instructions to tidy punctuation, change the tone or make a list. It works with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or your own server.

Choose Your Speech Engine

Start with your phone's built-in speech recognition, which is free and needs no key. If you want more accuracy or speed, connect OpenAI, Groq, Google Gemini, Deepgram or any OpenAI-compatible server with your own API key. Model names are editable, so new models work without waiting for an app update.

Start It Your Way

Use the floating bubble, the Dictate button in the notification, the Dictate tile in Quick Settings, or the big button inside the app. Long-press the bubble for a quick menu to switch AI rewrite on or off. You can also keep your last 50 dictations, with their AI versions, on the phone.
