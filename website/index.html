Floating microphone

One bubble. Every app.

There's no keyboard to switch and nothing to copy and paste. The Speaker bubble stays at the edge of your screen, ready whenever you need it.

  • Tap to start, tap again to stop. Drag it anywhere.
  • Words are typed where your cursor is, phrase by phrase.
  • Keeps listening through pauses, so you can stop and think.
  • If an app doesn't accept typing, your words go to the clipboard instead.

AI rewrite

Say it your way. Send it polished.

Turn on AI rewrite and Speaker collects what you say in a small draft card instead of typing it straight away. Read it, fix a word, then send the whole thing at once.

  • The whole draft goes to the AI in one go, so it keeps the full context.
  • Tap the text in the card to edit it before sending.
  • Write your own prompt: tidy punctuation, formal tone, bullet points, translation.
  • Works with OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq or your own server.

How it works

Ready in a couple of minutes

Install the app, allow a few permissions, and start talking.

1

Install and set up

Download Speaker and follow the short setup card: microphone, the floating bubble and auto-typing.

2

Pick an engine

Start with your phone's free built-in recognition, or add a key for OpenAI, Groq, Gemini or Deepgram.

3

Tap and talk

Open any app, tap into a text box, tap the bubble and speak. Tap again when you're done.

Speech engines

Use the engine that suits you

Speaker works with your phone's own speech recognition or with the leading speech services, using your own key. You can change model names any time, so new models work without an app update.

Built for everyday use

Small details that add up

Quick Settings tile

Add the Dictate tile and start from the notification shade in any app.

Notification controls

A Dictate / Stop button is always one swipe away while Speaker runs.

Recent dictations

Optionally keep your last 50 dictations and their AI versions on the phone, ready to copy.

Quick menu

Long-press the bubble to switch AI rewrite on or off, or jump to the app.

Privacy

Your words stay between you and the services you choose

Speaker has no servers. We never receive your voice, your text or your keys.

No account

No sign-up, no email address, no profile. Install it and use it.

Keys stay on your phone

API keys live in the app's private storage, are left out of backups, and only go to their own provider.

No ads or tracking

No analytics, no advertising SDKs, nothing running in the background to profile you.
