Welcome to Speaker

Speaker puts a microphone over every app on your Android phone. Tap it, speak naturally, and your words are typed right where you're writing. No keyboard switching, no copy and paste.

What Speaker does
Floating microphoneA bubble floats over every app. Tap to start, tap again to stop, drag it anywhere.
Types where your cursor isWords go straight into the text box you're using. If an app won't accept typing, they go to the clipboard.
AI rewrite (optional)Your words collect in a draft card. Edit them, tap Send, and the clean version is typed in.
Your choice of engineFree built-in recognition, or OpenAI, Groq, Gemini or Deepgram with your own key.
Many ways to startThe bubble, the notification button, the Quick Settings tile or the button in the app.
Private by designNo account, no ads, no analytics. We never receive your voice, text or keys.
How it works
  1. Install Speaker and follow the short setup card: microphone, the floating bubble and auto-typing.
  2. Open any app and tap into a text box.
  3. Tap the bubble and talk. Each phrase is typed as you pause. Tap again to stop.
Tip: AI rewriteWith AI rewrite on, nothing is typed while you talk. Your words collect in a small card beside the bubble, where you can fix them. Tap Send (or the bubble) and the whole text is rewritten at once, then typed in. If no text box is selected, it's copied to the clipboard.

Your words stay yours

Speaker has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your voice goes either to your phone's own speech service or straight to the provider you chose. API keys stay in the app's private storage on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
