Frequently Asked Questions

Installing

Why isn't Speaker on Google Play?
It's on its way. Until then you can install it from this site. When the Google Play version arrives, this page will link to it.
My phone says the file or app may be harmful. Is it safe?
Android shows that warning for every app installed from outside Google Play. Speaker has no ads, no trackers and no servers, and only asks for the permissions it needs to work. You can check the file against the fingerprints on the download page.
How do I update?
Download the new version from the download page and install it over the old one. Don't uninstall first, so you keep your settings and keys.
Can I move to the Google Play version later?
Yes. If Android won't install the Play version over this one, uninstall Speaker first, then install it from Play. You'll need to enter your keys and settings again.

Using Speaker

My words go to the clipboard instead of the text box. Why?
That means auto-typing is off. Open Settings → Accessibility → Speaker auto-type and switch it on again. If it keeps switching off by itself, your phone is closing Speaker in the background: see the next question.
The bubble disappears, or auto-typing keeps turning off.
Some phones close background apps to save battery, and Android then turns off that app's Accessibility service too. Set Speaker's battery use to Unrestricted, turn on Auto launch or Autostart if your phone has it, and lock Speaker in the recent-apps screen. The setup guide has details.
The auto-type switch is greyed out.
That's Android 13's protection for apps installed from a website. Go to Settings → Apps → Speaker → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again. See step 2 of the guide.
Auto-typing doesn't work in one particular app.
A few apps, such as some terminals and games, don't use standard text boxes. In those apps Speaker copies your words to the clipboard. Long-press in the app and choose Paste.
Does dictation stop when I go quiet?
No. It keeps listening until you tap the bubble again, so you can pause to think.
Which languages work?
That depends on the engine. Leave Language on auto-detect, or type a code such as en, hi or en-IN. The built-in engine supports the languages installed for your phone's speech recognition.

Costs and privacy

Is Speaker really free?
Yes. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. The built-in engine costs nothing. If you choose an online engine or AI rewrite, you use your own key and pay that provider directly, often a few cents or less for a lot of dictation. Some providers have free tiers.
Do you see what I say?
No. Speaker has no servers. Your voice goes either to your phone's own speech service or straight from your phone to the provider you chose. See the privacy policy.
Why does Speaker need Accessibility?
That is the Android feature that lets an app type into text boxes in other apps, the way a keyboard does. Speaker only uses it to type your dictated words. It doesn't read or collect what's on your screen.
Where are my API keys kept?
Only in Speaker's private storage on your phone. They're left out of backups and device transfers, and each key is only sent to the provider it belongs to.
